Create one exceptionally polished logo exploration presentation board for BRIEFLY, a Korean daily news briefing product that seamlessly connects reading and listening. The client strongly disliked a previous double-quotation-mark logo. This is a NEW exploration, not a modification of that logo.

Deliver ONE landscape logo contact sheet with SIX genuinely distinct logo directions in a precise 3-column by 2-row grid. Pure white background, generous whitespace, fine light gray separators, equally sized visual presentations, restrained tiny letter labels A–F at the upper left of each cell. All logos use identical single solid cobalt blue #244BEB, so shape and typography can be compared fairly. No gradients, no shadows, no textures, no photography, no glossy mockups, no fake interface screens. Flat, extremely crisp vector-like graphic design. Design quality of a specialist independent identity studio. Strong optical balancing, professional lettering, no clipart.

Each cell contains one large horizontal logo lockup in its center and, below it, a small consistent cobalt app-icon square with a white version of the corresponding identifying mark. Keep clear separation between the six concepts. Main name must read exactly "briefly" in lower case except concept C which uses exactly "BRIEFLY". Spell carefully b-r-i-e-f-l-y. No taglines, no explanatory paragraphs, no invented words. Labels below the letter may be only these English concept names:
A "Open B"
B "Editorial"
C "Compact"
D "Fold"

Art direction for each concept:
A Open B: a sculptural lowercase b monogram constructed from two broad open-page planes with one clean negative-space cut; an exceptionally simple, weighty, ownable silhouette. Pair with a confident neutral rounded-grotesk briefly wordmark. Avoid the generic filled B in a circle.
B Editorial: a refined custom lowercase briefly wordmark with contemporary flared serif details and beautiful tight-but-legible spacing. Make the b and f particularly distinctive but highly readable. Typographic logo, no separate symbol in the main lockup; its distinctive b serves as the small icon. Contemporary news culture, not luxury cosmetics.
C Compact: bespoke condensed uppercase BRIEFLY logotype with a strong dense rhythm and precisely cut terminals. Design an integrated B/F monogram as its app-icon extraction. Sharp and urban, readable, bold, not italic sports branding.
D Fold: a unique reduced folded-page emblem made from two interlocking angular planes, subtly suggesting both a bookmark and lowercase b. Not a literal document icon. Pair with a restrained humanist lowercase briefly wordmark.
E Wordmark: a memorable soft geometric lowercase briefly logotype with clever bespoke connected f/l construction, open counters, a controlled square i dot, subtly shortened terminal strokes. No additional symbol beside the word. The custom b is its app icon. Friendly and contemporary without looking childish.
F Continuity: a beautiful single continuous ribbon forming a lowercase b with a subtle open-ended loop representing a story carried from reading to listening. Broad continuous strokes, not thin linework, not an infinity sign. Pair with an elegant, open lowercase briefly wordmark.

Important: no quotation marks anywhere in the logo designs, no double quotes, no generic play-button triangles, no microphone/headphone icons, no newspaper clipart, no lightning bolts, no AI sparkles, no copied famous brand marks. Show six different silhouettes and letter treatments, NOT six variations of one mark. Let the typographic directions B, C, E be visibly different from the symbol-plus-wordmark directions A, D, F. This is a designer's decision board, not a brand kit with many unrelated panels.
